How to play

  1. Make a private room and share the code.
  2. Press 🎉 Party and pick Speed Race.
  3. Everyone starts on the same article with the same target.
  4. Click links as fast as you can. First to the target wins.

Rules

  • Only time counts; extra clicks and Back presses cost nothing but seconds.
  • Every finishing route is checked against Wikipedia.
  • You have 5 minutes.

Tips

💡 Don’t read, skim: headings and the contents list are your friends.
💡 Jump to a big hub article fast, then narrow down.
💡 Going back is free in Speed Race, so don’t be afraid to try a link.
