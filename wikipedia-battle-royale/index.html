Battle Royale

Battle Royale turns a Wikipedia race into a knockout tournament. Everyone races, the last-placed player is knocked out, and you keep going round after round until only one champion is left.

Play Battle Royale

How to play

  1. Make a private room with at least 3 players.
  2. Press 🎉 Party and pick Battle Royale.
  3. Race as normal. When the round ends, last place is knocked out.
  4. Knocked-out players can spectate the rest live.
  5. Keep starting rounds until one player is left: they win.

Rules

  • Needs 3 to 8 players.
  • Ranking is fewest clicks, then fastest time. Giving up or running out of time puts you at the bottom.
  • Each round has 5 minutes.

Tips

  • Playing safe beats taking risks early: you only need to avoid last place.
  • In the final rounds every click matters, so slow down and plan.
  • Watching others while knocked out is a great way to learn shortcuts.
