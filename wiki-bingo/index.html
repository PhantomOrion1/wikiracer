Wiki Bingo

Wiki Bingo is a Wikipedia race with a twist: instead of one target, everyone gets the same 3×3 bingo card of articles. Click through Wikipedia to visit them, and the first player to complete a full line wins.

How to play

  1. Make a private room and share the code with friends.
  2. Press 🎉 Party and pick Wiki Bingo.
  3. Everyone starts on the same article with the same 3×3 card.
  4. Tap a square to make it your target and read what it is. Change it whenever you like.
  5. Visiting a page on the card marks it off. Complete any row, column or diagonal to win.

Rules

  • Clicks don’t matter, only speed: the first full line wins.
  • Every route is checked against Wikipedia, so you have to really visit the squares.
  • You have 10 minutes.

Tips

💡 Look for a line where the topics share a hub page, like a country or a decade.
💡 Big general articles (countries, centuries, sciences) link to almost everything.
💡 Switch targets when you spot a shortcut to a different square.
