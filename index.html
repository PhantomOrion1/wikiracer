How to play

  1. 1
    Start

    You land on a Wikipedia article.

  2. 2
    Click links

    Only links inside the article. No search bar.

  3. 3
    Reach the target

    Fewest clicks wins. Ties go to the fastest time.

Time limits
Easy 2:30Medium 5:00Hard 10:00
What's a click?

Every page you open counts, including the ← Back button.

Not allowed
  • Leaving the tab hides the article (the clock keeps running)
  • Word search (Ctrl/⌘+F) is switched off
  • External, category and file links don't work
Multiplayer
  • Up to 8 players per room
  • Every route is checked against Wikipedia
  • Done early? Spectate or play Lane Rush
  • If the host leaves, the next player takes over
