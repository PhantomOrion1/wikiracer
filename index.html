How to play
  • Start on one article and reach the target using only links inside the article. Search, external links, and category or file pages don't work.
  • Every page change is one click, including the in-game Back button. Fewest clicks wins, and ties go to the fastest time.
  • Time limits: 2:30 easy, 5:00 medium, 10:00 hard.
  • Stay on the tab. The first time you leave you get a warning. After that, every time you leave adds a +1 click penalty.
  • In multiplayer the host checks every route against Wikipedia. Routes with fake clicks are flagged.
  • Finished early? Spectate the others live, or play Flappy Wiki while you wait.
