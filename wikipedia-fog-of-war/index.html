Fog of War

In Fog of War you race through Wikipedia, but only the intro of each article is visible. The rest of the page is hidden, so you have to guess which links will get you closer to the target.

Volcano

A volcano is an opening in the Earth’s crust where magma, ash and gas escape. Many sit along the Ring of Fire.

Players2–8
Time10:00
WinnerFewest clicks

How to play

  1. Make a private room and invite friends.
  2. Press 🎉 Party and pick Fog of War.
  3. Everyone races from the same start to the same target.
  4. Each article shows only its intro, so choose your links carefully.

Rules

  • Fewest clicks wins; ties go to the fastest time.
  • You can still use the Back button (it counts as a click).
  • You have 10 minutes, and the pairs are a bit easier to make up for the fog.

Tips

💡 Intros usually link to the broadest related topics, which make great stepping stones.
💡 If a page is a dead end, go back early rather than wandering.
💡 Read the target’s description on the side panel to guess which subject it belongs to.
