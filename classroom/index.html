For teachers

A wiki race gets students reading closely, scanning for relevant information and explaining how ideas connect, while it feels like a game. WikiRacer Live is free, has no ads, and needs no student accounts or emails.

Open WikiRacerRules for students

What students practise

  • Skimming and scanning: finding the useful sentence fast instead of reading every word.
  • Categorising: recognising that a topic belongs to a bigger field, place or period.
  • Reasoning and planning: working backwards from a goal, then explaining the route.
  • Vocabulary and general knowledge: every race passes through unfamiliar articles.

Running a race with your class

  1. Create a private room and put the 5-letter code on the board. Each room holds up to 8 players, so for a big class run several rooms, or let groups share a device and race as teams.
  2. Choose the pair yourself: pick Custom and type the start and target articles. Linking two topics from your current unit works well ("Photosynthesis" to "Industrial Revolution").
  3. Race. Everyone starts together after a 3-second countdown. Fewest clicks wins; ties go to the faster time.
  4. Debrief with the results screen. It shows every player's full route. Ask students to justify their key click: why did that link get them closer?

Lesson ideas

  • Unit connector: start on this week's topic and finish on last week's.
  • Predict first: students write down their planned route before racing, then compare.
  • Fewest clicks challenge: no time pressure. Can anyone beat the class record for a pair?
  • Daily warm-up: the daily challenge gives everyone the same pair; share results at the start of class.

Things to know

  • Content comes straight from Wikipedia. Articles are unfiltered, so supervise younger students and choose pairs that keep them in suitable areas.
  • Names: students type any display name. Obvious slurs are filtered automatically; ask students to use first names or nicknames.
  • Privacy: no accounts, no cookies and no ads. See the privacy page for exactly what is collected. The site isn't directed at children under 13.
  • Devices: any modern browser works, including iPads, Chromebooks and phones. Leaving the tab mid-race adds a penalty, which keeps attention on the task.
